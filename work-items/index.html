What does a work item actually require?

Source-referenced components, quantities, crew categories and calculation gates for construction scheduling. Unlike a typical online rate table, this library never silently substitutes a national benchmark for a particular site.

Evidence boundary: Delhi CPWD masonry coefficients are reference examples, not Nepal-approved productivity. The other method templates deliberately contain no invented coefficients. A crew-day result is a theoretical role-capacity lower bound—it excludes access, sequencing, inspection holds, rework, weather and actual site productivity.

Reference hierarchy and what we still need

Priority 1: a completed site's measured quantity, actual person-hours, equipment hours, weather and site method, all linked to an immutable record. Priority 2: explicitly identified national norms and manufacturer specifications that match the exact work description. Priority 3: transparently stated planning assumptions (never auto-approved).
