# Construction Project Master Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
**Version:** 1.0 (Synthetic Baseline)
**Target:** GitHub Actions Scheduling Engine Input

This document defines a Primavera P6-style hierarchical WBS for a standard commercial/residential construction project, mapped from initial client contact to final handover. 

## WBS Level 1: Project Lifecycle
*   **1000** - Project Initiation & Planning
*   **2000** - Architectural & Engineering Design
*   **3000** - Permitting & Regulatory Approvals
*   **4000** - Pre-Construction & Site Preparation
*   **5000** - Substructure (Foundation & Below Grade)
*   **6000** - Superstructure (Shell & Core)
*   **7000** - Enclosure & Exterior Finishes
*   **8000** - MEP Rough-ins (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing)
*   **9000** - Interior Fit-Out & Finishes
*   **10000** - Testing, Commissioning & Handover

---

## Detailed Task Breakdown

### 1000: Project Initiation & Planning
*   **1010:** Initial Client Consultation & Requirements Gathering
*   **1020:** Site Feasibility Study & Surveying
*   **1030:** Budget Estimation & Project Charter Approval
*   **1040:** **Milestone:** Project Kickoff

### 2000: Architectural & Engineering Design
*   **2010:** Conceptual Design & Client Review
*   **2020:** Schematic Design (SD) Development
*   **2030:** Design Development (DD) & Structural Engineering
*   **2040:** MEP System Engineering Design
*   **2050:** Construction Documents (CD) Finalization (Legalized Drawings)
*   **2060:** **Milestone:** Design Freeze & Drawings Baseline

### 3000: Permitting & Regulatory Approvals
*   **3010:** Environmental & Zoning Clearances
*   **3020:** Submit Legalized Drawings for Governmental Review
*   **3030:** Address Regulatory Revisions & Resubmit
*   **3040:** Obtain Building & Utility Permits
*   **3050:** **Milestone:** Permits Secured (Ready for Construction)

### 4000: Pre-Construction & Site Preparation
*   **4010:** Contractor Mobilization & Temporary Facilities Setup
*   **4020:** Site Clearing, Grubbing & Demolition (if required)
*   **4030:** Establish Site Survey Control & Set Out Building Grid

### 5000: Substructure
*   **5010:** Bulk Excavation & Earthwork
*   **5020:** Shoring & Retaining Walls (if applicable)
*   **5030:** Formwork, Rebar, and Pour Footings / Pile Caps
*   **5040:** Below-Grade Waterproofing & Drainage
*   **5050:** Pour Slab-on-Grade (SOG)
*   **5060:** **Milestone:** Substructure Complete

### 6000: Superstructure
*   **6010:** Erect Columns & Shear Walls (Ground to Roof)
*   **6020:** Form, Reinforce, and Pour Suspended Slabs
*   **6030:** Roof Structural Framing (Steel/Concrete)
*   **6040:** **Milestone:** Superstructure Complete (Topping Out)

### 7000: Enclosure & Exterior Finishes
*   **7010:** Exterior Masonry / Facade Framing
*   **7020:** Roof Waterproofing & Covering
*   **7030:** Install Windows, Glazing & Exterior Doors
*   **7040:** **Milestone:** Building Dried-In (Weather-tight)

### 8000: MEP Rough-ins
*   **8010:** HVAC Ductwork & Equipment Rough-in
*   **8020:** Plumbing Water & Waste Lines Rough-in
*   **8030:** Electrical Conduit & Wiring Rough-in
*   **8040:** MEP Rough-in Inspections (Government/Local Authority)

### 9000: Interior Fit-Out & Finishes
*   **9010:** Insulation & Drywall Installation
*   **9020:** Taping, Mudding & Primary Painting
*   **9030:** Hard Flooring & Tile Installation
*   **9040:** MEP Trim (Fixtures, Switches, Registers)
*   **9050:** Millwork, Cabinetry & Doors
*   **9060:** Final Coat Painting & Soft Flooring

### 10000: Testing, Commissioning & Handover
*   **10010:** MEP Systems Testing & Commissioning
*   **10020:** Final Governmental Inspections & Occupancy Certificate
*   **10030:** Snagging / Punch List Walkthrough with Client
*   **10040:** Final Deep Cleaning of Site
*   **10050:** **Milestone:** Substantial Completion & Key Submission
