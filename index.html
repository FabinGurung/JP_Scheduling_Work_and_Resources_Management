What about actual site progress?

No site-progress data has been connected to this demonstration. We cannot truthfully label jobs “done”, “late”, “in progress” or “ready to start” without dated observations, required approvals, and dependency evidence.

Once authenticated progress inputs are connected, this view can highlight only verified next actions and blocked work.

Where does this roadmap come from?

The WBS and durations originate in two synthetic Google Drive source documents. GitHub Actions runs deterministic CPM, checks the results independently, records hashes, and publishes the verified output.
